## Correlation
Correlation is statistical method used for determining in what way two variables are dependent.
Positive correlation means there are parameters that do impact one another.
Negative correlation means there are parameters that do impact one another but they are reverse.
No correlation means there are no common parameters.

Strenght correlation messuring:
Correlation coefficient:
If the value is close to +1 the correlation is positive.
If the value is close to -1 the correlation is negative.
If the value is close to 0 there is no correlation.

P-value:
P-value < 0.001 means there is strong certainty that the parameters have strong certinty of being correlated.
P-value < 0.05 means moderate certainty in the result.
P-value < 0.1 means there is weak certainty in the result.

Generally the strong correlation is one that has correlation coefficient close to 1 or -1 and P-value less then 0.001.
